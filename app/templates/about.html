About ServiceLink

Our Mission

ServiceLink is a web-based platform designed to connect local service providers with clients who need their services. Whether you're looking for a cleaner, handyman, tutor, or any other service professional, ServiceLink makes it easy to find, book, and review qualified service providers in your area.

How It Works

For Clients:
  1. Create an account and complete your profile
  2. Search for services by category and location
  3. Browse provider profiles and read reviews
  4. Book available time slots with your chosen provider
  5. Leave a review after the service is completed
For Service Providers:
  1. Register as a service provider
  2. Create and manage your service listings
  3. Set your availability and pricing
  4. Receive and manage booking requests
  5. Build your reputation through client reviews

Key Features

  • User Authentication: Secure registration and login system
  • Role-Based Access: Separate dashboards for clients and providers
  • Service Discovery: Search and filter services by category and location
  • Booking System: Easy-to-use booking with availability management
  • Reviews & Ratings: Transparent feedback system for completed services
  • Dashboard Management: Track bookings and manage services
  • Secure Platform: Password hashing and CSRF protection
